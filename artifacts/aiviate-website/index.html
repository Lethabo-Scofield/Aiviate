Safety on the road

A tired driver is a risk on every road. Aiviate checks in before the trip and looks after the driver while they drive.

Pre-drive fatigue check-in

  • Four quick questions before the first route: sleep, alertness, hours awake, and anything else making the driver tired
  • Drowsy medication, illness, skipped meals, stress, a late shift or alcohol all count
  • Medium or high risk alerts the dispatcher, so they can plan rest or a handover

Anti-sleep earpiece

  • Alerts a driver who is getting drowsy
  • Online, battery and readiness at a glance
  • Paired to one driver from the Devices page

Dashcam MLplanned

  • Machine learning to spot unsafe driving
  • Will pair from the same page

Ready for take-off

How to use Aiviate on day one. Both apps also have a guided tour and a Learn page, so nobody is left on the runway.

For dispatchers

  1. Sign in. Create your company account, or press Try the demo. A one-minute tour starts automatically. Replay it any time from your profile menu → Take the tour.
  2. Add your drivers. Go to Drivers → Add Driver. Aiviate creates their login: give each driver their email and the temporary password it shows.
  3. Bring in orders. Sync your store, or use Upload & Optimize to drop in a spreadsheet.
  4. Dispatch. Assign the optimised routes. They appear on each driver's phone straight away.
  5. Keep watch. Fatigue alerts and exceptions come to you. Post notices to every driver in Community.

For drivers

  1. Sign in. Your company would have given you a login email and a temporary password, or an activation link by email. Use those in the Aiviate Driver app.
  2. Take the tour. It runs once when you first sign in. Replay it from Profile → Replay app tour.
  3. Check in before you drive. Answer four questions about sleep and tiredness. Honest answers never count against you; they help dispatch keep you safe.
  4. Accept and deliver. Accept a route, follow the stops in order, scan and complete each delivery.
  5. Talk to the team. Share road updates and safety warnings in Community, and find guides in Learn.
